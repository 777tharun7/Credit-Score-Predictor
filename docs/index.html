Website Rules, Policies & Governance

All users, lending officers, and researchers must follow these platform rules when using Creovate.

Platform Rules Acknowledgement

Confirm that you have read and understood the educational, privacy, and verification rules below.

Rule 01 • Scope

Educational & Non-Lending Prototype

  • This application is an academic and research prototype built for demonstration.
  • Predicted credit scores and eligibility flags must not be used to approve, reject, price, or deny real-world credit or welfare benefits.
  • The eligibility threshold is derived dynamically from the dataset median (~619).
Rule 02 • Access & Auth

Authentication & Account Security

  • Access to the live Credit Score Predictor requires an authenticated session.
  • Passwords must be at least 8 characters and are hashed using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with per-user salts.
  • Do not share personal credentials; use the provided Demo accounts for quick evaluation.
Rule 03 • Privacy

Data Privacy & PII Protection

  • Never upload real applicant Aadhaar numbers, bank statements, government IDs, or private financial records.
  • Uploaded applicant images are previewed locally in your browser and are not stored on any external server.
  • Assessment history is scoped strictly to the signed-in user account.
Rule 04 • Verification

Income & Document Verification Rules

  • Combined declared income sums annual_income, joint_income, and business_income.
  • If Document Validity is Expired or Not Provided, or ID Verified is No, the assessment is automatically flagged for manual review.
  • Loan-to-Income ratios exceeding 5.0x trigger an automatic affordability warning.
Rule 05 • Fairness

Algorithmic Fairness & Transparency

  • Sensitive identity attributes (name, gender, region, user_id) are excluded from the ANN inference vector.
  • Every prediction includes a per-feature impact breakdown so decisions remain auditable and explainable.
  • Human-in-the-loop review is mandatory whenever verification flags are raised.
Rule 06 • Acceptable Use

Input Validation & Responsible Conduct

  • All numeric inputs must be non-negative; age must be between 18 and 100, and repayment score between 0 and 1000.
  • Automated scraping, brute-force login attempts, or tampering with model artifacts is prohibited.
  • Report any unexpected model behavior or data anomalies to the Creovate team.
